The Dakini Sanctuary

A Garden Cafe Suspended in Mountain Clouds

Tucked away on Jogiwara Road, just moments from Ani Gompa in McLeod Ganj, **Dakini House** is an ethereal sanctuary where culinary artistry harmonizes with the grandeur of the Himalayas.

Whether you are sipping our signature espresso while watching sunlight shift across the snow-clad Dhauladhar ridges, or indulging in our chef-crafted Italian plates by the fireplace in our charming wooden interiors, every moment at Dakini House is designed to soothe the soul.
